    About the model

    Transfer learning,
    trained on real fruit.

    This site runs a VGG16 convolutional network fine-tuned with PyTorch on 16 fruit categories — including a dedicated no fruit class. Images are normalized with ImageNet statistics and passed through the network exactly as during validation.

    224 × 224 RGB

    Images are EXIF-corrected, resized and center-cropped before inference.

    FastAPI inference

    The trained checkpoint loads once at startup and serves predictions over HTTP.

    16 fruit classes

    Apple, banana, cherry, chickoo, coconut, dates, grapes, guava, kiwi, mango and more.
